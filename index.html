The Story

Read the text once quickly. Then read it again slowly before you answer the questions below.

"He didn't just sing a song — he told you a story." — how fans described Frank Sinatra's voice.

Francis Albert Sinatra was born on 12 December 1915, in Hoboken, New Jersey. His parents were Italian immigrantsиммигранты, and life was not always easy for the family. But young Frank loved music from an early age. He left school early and worked hard to become a singer. In 1939, he got his first big chance: he joined a band led by the trumpeter Harry James.

A year later, Sinatra started singing with Tommy Dorsey's orchestra, and his career took offстремительно стал успешным. By the early 1940s, he was already a star. Teenage girls, known as "bobby soxers," screamed and cried at his concerts. People started calling him "The Voice" because of his smooth, emotional singing style.

Sinatra's career was not always successful, though. In the late 1940s, his popularityпопулярность dropped, and some people thought his best days were over. But in 1953, he won an Academy Award for his acting in the film From Here to Eternity. This role changed everything and brought him back to the top.

Over the years, Sinatra became famous not only as a singer but also as an actor. He appearedпоявился / снялся in more than 50 films and recorded hundreds of songs, including classics like "My Way" and "New York, New York." He was also part of a famous group of entertainersартисты called the "Rat Pack," together with Dean Martin and Sammy Davis Jr. They often performed together on stage in Las Vegas.

During his long career, Sinatra had many nicknames: "Ol' Blue Eyes," "The Chairman of the Board," and "The Voice." He sold more than 150 million records around the world and won several Grammy Awards. Frank Sinatra died in 1998, but his music is still popular today, and many people believe he was one of the greatest singers of the 20th century.

Focus

Grammar: Past Simple

Telling a life story

Biographies like this one mostly use the Past Simple tense to describe finished actions and events in someone's life:

He joined Harry James's band in 1939. He won an Academy Award in 1953. He died in 1998.

Try it: Go back to the reading text and underline five more verbs in the Past Simple. Then write three true sentences about your own life using the Past Simple tense.
